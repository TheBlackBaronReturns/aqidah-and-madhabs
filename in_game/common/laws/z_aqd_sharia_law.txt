# =====================================================================
# SYSTEM B - decouple the madhhab law (vanilla sharia_law) from
# religious_school.
#
# Vanilla makes each madhhab policy selectable only when your
# religious_school is that madhhab (or a non-madhhab), and the law is
# "changed through the main school" (change_main_school). After System B
# the religious_school slot is creed-only, so:
#   - the law is locked to direct editing (locked = always) and changed
#     only through the deliberate aqd_change_madhhab action;
#   - each policy potential drops the religious_school ?= { NOR ... }
#     sub-clause, so any madhhab of the sect is a legal target;
#   - the modifiers the evicted *_school entries carried (incl. the
#     mysticism/jurisprudence axis pushes) are folded into the policies.
# =====================================================================
